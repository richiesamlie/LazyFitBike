## Numbered Findings List

1. **[HIGH] [R-35]** `spectator-web/app/` is missing a `favicon.ico` or `icon.svg`, causing a `404 (Not Found)` console error when loading `http://localhost:3000/`.
2. **[HIGH] [R-25]** `app/src/main/java/com/fitness/zwiftbike/MainActivity.kt` (`OutlinedTextField` in Map, Strava, Profile, and Workout modals) inherits `Theme.Material.Light.NoActionBar` (`themes.xml`) without explicit `OutlinedTextFieldDefaults.colors(...)`, risking low unfocused label/border contrast on `#0B1510` and `#13221A` dark surfaces.
3. **[HIGH] [R-25]** `wear/src/main/java/com/fitness/zwiftbike/wear/MainActivity.kt` (`isStealthBatterySaver` eco screen) uses `#52525B` and `#3F3F46` dim text on `#000000` black for AMOLED power savings, which falls below the 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast floor while eco mode is active.
4. **[HIGH] [R-27]** `app/src/main/assets/world3d.html` (`#fullmap-container` 2-tap custom route builder) displays a text hint while fetching OSRM or Google Routes v2 directions, without a visual loading indicator or request-failure retry state.
5. **[HIGH] [R-35]** `:app` Compose modals (`MainActivity.kt`), `CanvaShareCardGenerator.kt`, and `:wear` (`wear/MainActivity.kt`) were verified via Gradle Kotlin compilation, unit tests, and APK assembly rather than an interactive Android Emulator or physical Galaxy Watch 4 UI click-through.
6. **[MEDIUM] [R-19]** `app/src/main/assets/world3d.html` (`updateWorldGeometry`) clamps `relD = Math.max(0, d)` on `guardrailsGroup` segments behind the camera (`d < 0`), producing a slight visual step seam on the foreground-left guardrail edge during curves or climbs.
